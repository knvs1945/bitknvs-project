About PKTB

PKTB stands for Pokémon Team Builder — a for-fun LangChain project built for bitknvs.com by Kevin Joseff Cabrera.

Search for any Pokémon and add up to six to your roster, then hit "Rate my team" to send it off to an AI agent for evaluation. It'll break down which types your team is strong and weak against, let you dig into how each individual Pokémon fits in, and even take a guess at what your picks say about your personality as a trainer.

Built with: vanilla HTML, CSS & JavaScript on the frontend; Flask, LangChain, Anthropic's Claude, and ChromaDB on the backend; PokéAPI for Pokémon data; Chart.js and html2canvas for the visuals.
